# ISSUE CONTRACT

## Pain
Operational hold evidence can collapse into prose, losing machine codes, priority, ownership, exact residual details, and the policy that selected the primary reason.

## Success
- Compile explicit residuals into one deterministic structured hold brief.
- Preserve stable subsystem:code identities, severity, details and owner mapping.
- Deduplicate identical subsystem/code residuals deterministically without losing equal-severity detail.
- Rank by explicit severity + subsystem-priority policy and bind that policy into the brief receipt.
- Preserve unknown subsystems visibly as unassigned instead of dropping them.
- Refuse malformed subsystem/code/detail/severity inputs.

## Boundary
This compiler explains supplied hold residuals. It does **not** decide whether a mission is safe, clear a hold, issue a go/no-go command, actuate hardware, authenticate upstream residuals, or claim SpaceX operational use.
